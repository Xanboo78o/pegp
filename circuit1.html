THE MAP

TRACK MAP — to scale (grid squares = 25 ft)

Lap ≈ 600 ft. Expected lap time 50–60 s at race pace. Recommend HEAT LAPS = 3 in the paddock settings for this layout — 4+ is finale-grade cardio.

THE CORNERS

CORNER GUIDE

  • T1 · THE ELBOW — fast right sweeper following the field's 25° kink, entered off the main straight with a slight downhill drift. Taken at nearly full pace on the wide line. First real overtaking look of the lap: outbrake into the entry.
  • T2 · THE HOOK — the hairpin at the far end of the dogleg. Slowest point on the circuit. Cardboard tire-wall on the outside. Prime divebomb territory — and prime switchback territory for whoever defends it.
  • T3 · THE SLIDE — downhill left rejoining the main field. You arrive with T2 exit speed and the slope pulling you wide toward the buffer. Discipline corner: greedy exits earn track-limits calls.
  • T4 · THE FLICK — noodle-gate chicane mid-way down the low straight. Breaks the straight into two overtaking zones. Cutting it is the classic corner-cut crime.
  • T5 · THE WALL — uphill 180° right. The boss fight. Late apex it and carry everything you can, because the exit climbs to the start/finish straight and the hill eats the unworthy. Tire-wall on the inside. This corner decides the lap.

⚠ HAZARD PROTOCOL — before first cone: walk the field and flag every utility pole and every guy-wire anchor. Wire anchors are invisible at sprint speed. If any cone lands within 4 paces of a pole or wire, slide the whole corner along until clear. Poles near the track get the pool-noodle wrap. The wire itself: track never goes near it. Ever.
🛡 THE BUFFER LAW — the 6 paces of grass along the road is not track, not runoff, not shortcut. Nothing lives there. All runoff aims uphill or along the field. The downhill section runs PARALLEL to the road by design — never re-angle it toward the curb.
KIT LIST

WHAT TO BRING

  • ~36 cones (two cheap 20-packs) — corners 20, kerb/edge markers 12, S/F + pit 4
  • 8–10 pool noodles — low-straight kerb, chicane gates, pole wrap
  • 6–10 cardboard boxes — tire walls at T2 + T5, water bottle inside each, sponsor paint strongly encouraged
  • Flag kit — green, yellow, red, white, checkered (see Racing School lesson 6)
  • Race Control — laptop or phone, charged, volume UP
  • Teardown — everything above fits in one bin. Field returns to boring by dinner.
