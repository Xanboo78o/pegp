THE MAP

TRACK MAP — same orientation as the satellite photo (road left · trees right · Eagles at the bottom)

Lap ≈ 465 ft — expect 40–50 s laps. This is the LONG layout — the endurance track. Save it for the Pembroke 10 and big-lung days; sprint rounds live on THE FIGURE 8. The two long lanes share the east corridor, split by the cone divider — dragstrip style. The gardens guard the road side; the track never goes west of them.

THE CORNERS

CORNER GUIDE

  • T1 — flat-out left kink off the main straight into the top pocket. Brave in quali, chaotic on lap 1 of a heat.
  • T2 · THE HOOK — hairpin in the dogleg pocket, box wall between you and the top road. Divebomb spot #1. Slowest point on the lap.
  • T3 · FLOWERBOX — right-hand flick around Garden 1's corner, then you run its east face, mulch inches off your right wheels. Clipping the garden = track limits, and everyone can SEE the evidence in the flowerbed.
  • T4 · GARDEN GATE — commit right, diving west into the canyon between Garden 1 and Garden 2. Downhill entry between two mulch walls. The signature corner.
  • T5 · THE WELL — the deep hairpin at the bottom of the gate, uphill exit climbing back east between the gardens. The boss corner: carry speed out or the slope drinks you. Garden 2's north face is your exit wall.
  • T6 · EAGLES CORNER — long left hairpin at the south end, named for the street it refuses to touch. Box wall on the exit. Divebomb spot #2, and the last passing chance before S/F.
SETUP SHEET

LAYING IT OUT — everything measures from real objects (1 pace ≈ 2.5 ft)

WHATHOW
Buffer lines (LAW)6 paces in from EVERY curb — left road, top curve, Eagles. Cone every 10 paces. Nothing lives roadward of these, ever.
Main straight (north lane)5 paces out from the tree-row mulch, running the whole field. This is the widest, fastest lane.
Lane divider4 paces west of the main straight — noodle snakes + a cone every 10 paces, from level with Garden 1's south edge down to the Eagles entry.
T1 kink2 cones starting the left bend, level with the top of the tree row.
T2 Hook5-cone arc in the pocket, apex 7 paces in from the left buffer. Wall boxes between the arc and the top buffer.
T3 Flowerbox2 cones, 1 pace off Garden 1's northeast corner; the running lane hugs its east face.
T4 Garden GateGate = 2 cones, 1 pace south of Garden 1's south face. The corridor into the gap IS the track.
T5 Well4-cone hairpin arc in the NORTH half of the gap — apex 3 paces east of the left buffer. Exit lane runs 1–2 paces off Garden 2's north face, back east.
T6 Eagles5-cone arc; the southernmost cone stays at least 12 paces north of the Eagles curb. Wall boxes south of the arc.
S/F lineAcross the main straight, 8 paces north of Garden 2's north edge. Grid boxes stagger down the lane behind it.
Pit boxRight side of the main straight, tucked against the tree-row mulch just north of the Eagles exit. Enter after T6, rejoin before S/F.
Director's tableInfield, west of the divider, facing S/F (30 ft sightline). Cross the track only when it's cold.
Paddock + tarpsSouth pocket between Eagles Corner and the bottom buffer, next to Garden 3. Garden 3 lives inside the buffer — it's scenery, not track.

±3 ft on any cone is nothing — the SHAPE matters. Walk one lap before the first session; the Director's eye outranks this sheet.

⚠ HAZARD PROTOCOL — the satellite shows something pole-shaped mid-field on the south side of the Garden 1–2 gap (marked ⚠ on the map), right where the Well's exit runs. Verify it on foot. If it's a pole: noodle-wrap it and keep the exit lane north of it. Walk the top pocket for guy-wire anchors before T2 goes live. Wire anchors get flagged AND a cone line keeping the track clear — a cable is invisible at sprint speed.
🛡 THE BUFFER LAW — roads on THREE sides here. Six paces off every curb is sacred grass: not track, not runoff, not shortcut. The gardens guard the west side; the walls guard the hairpins; the buffer guards everything else. Downhill runs parallel to the road, never at it.
KIT LIST

WHAT TO BRING

  • ~50 cones (three cheap 20-packs) — corners 18, divider 14, buffers 12, S/F + grid + pit 6
  • 10–12 pool noodles — divider snakes, Well exit kerb, pole wrap
  • 6–10 cardboard boxes — walls at the Hook + Eagles, water bottle inside each, sponsor paint mandatory-ish
  • Flag kit — green, yellow, red, white, checkered (Racing School lesson 6)
  • Race Control — laptop or phone, charged, volume UP
  • Teardown — one bin. Field goes back to boring by dinner.
